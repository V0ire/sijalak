## Canonical Analysis and Decisions
- D-19/D-23: G0 names 02-01 before startup; contact/collection/site procedures and guardian consent plus child's current willingness are distinct prerequisites, not bundled permissions.
- D-20: baseline normal activity without robot first; neutral incident questions separate [O] behavior, [K] accounts, [I] inference and [?] unknown, including unaided starts and contrary findings.
- D-02/D-09: provisional 10–14/Bahasa Indonesia must be checked against actual visitor purpose, language, assistance and accessibility; no reading diagnosis or forced age quota.
- D-21: validate/revise ≥30s voluntary content engagement or assisted reading jointly; distinguish holding/opening, picture/shared reading, help and robot play. Behavior is not comprehension or habit proof.
- D-11/D-22: use 6–12 real available books, stable book IDs, source-linked suitability/availability and human/original/licensed content rights; book ownership is not copying permission.
- D-24: prepare matched books/cues/attention and invitation/exposure/opt-in denominators; no comparison of all baseline visitors with self-selected robot users and no impact claim from preferences.
- D-01/D-18: child content/field procedures use no Gemini, microphone capture or audio egress, including Gemini-written child scripts and forwarded speech; no parent/proxy/paid bypass.
- A: two 30–45min periods, operator 10–15min and 4–6 varied voluntary 3–5min conversations are scheduling defaults, revisable to permission/attendance, not existing encounters or required quotas.
