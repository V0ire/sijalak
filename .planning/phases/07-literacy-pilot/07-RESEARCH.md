## Pilot reasoning, not observed field status
- Keep child-local distinct from restricted adult D3: human-authored/original, human-recorded or licensed local hooks, no capture, Gemini generation, provider credentials or cloud-AI egress.
- Actual signed/deployment image, backend permission, adult-token removal/revocation and mic/no-egress checks across START/STOP/reboot must match the intended unit; a mode flag or checklist header cannot prove isolation.
- Accepted Phase2 procedures/baseline/6–12 real titles and Phase6 actual fit/power/thermal/acoustic/STOP evidence precede participant access; adult-demo success is neither child/site safety nor field-benefit evidence.
- Hook plus at most three options per step and voluntary original book choices keeps book handoff testable. Proposed≤90s handoff and≥30s book-content engagement, including assisted/shared reading, are targets/proxy rules, not comprehension measures.
- Cohort10–14 is provisional; site attendance, ability without diagnosis, language/access needs and collection criteria require actual approved evidence, not invented demographics.

## Future protocol and measurement gaps
- Proposed10–20 eligible opportunities per condition over≥2 periods (roughly20 at the upper planning end) is exploratory feasibility, not required recruitment, statistical power or efficacy. Do not force attendance to reach a count.
- Match books/invitation information/human attention/help in robot and no-robot card/relawan conditions; eligibility must not depend on robot opt-in. Counterbalance where feasible and disclose deviations/repeat-exposure uncertainty.
- Define eligible/invited/exposed/opt-in/refusal/start/help denominators, missingness and stop/continue/revise criteria before collection; zero denominators are not estimable, not favourable outcomes.
- Consent/guardian/ongoing assent, contact/site/body approval where applicable, notified observation and refusal without penalty are separate gates. No child debugging or hidden collection is authorized.
- Consent forms, identities and temporary notes remain operator-private outside the project under approved custody/retention/deletion; repository observations are disclosure-reviewed aggregates only after authorization, without identifying timestamps/quotes/links or exposed small cells.
- Check all active holdings/rights, offline START/STOP, maintenance/disable, corrupt/missing content and truthful local fallback; book ownership is not copying permission. Distress, heat, fixation, reader disruption or overload stops activity, not a pressure to finish quotas.
