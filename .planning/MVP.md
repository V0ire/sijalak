**Must show:** explicit PTT, expressive OLED state, real robot microphone→VPS/API→STT/chat/TTS→robot speaker, stop/reset/session isolation, bounded failure/cost behavior, usable protected enclosure and safe verified power.

## Provisional literacy MVP: local curated book starter
**Must show:** playful character, voluntary start/stop, short human-authored/recorded/licensed book hook, bounded choice of known available books, quiet handoff to reading, operator care, anonymous feasibility observation.
**Primary assumed cohort:**10–14, revise after observations. **Primary hypothesis:** helps start reading. No Gemini/cloud calls or voice capture from this child-local profile. Live child AI is blocked pending eligible provider/contract review, not silently proxied.
